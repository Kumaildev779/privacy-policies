Kode Card — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Contact: malikayazmalik45@gmail.com

1. OVERVIEW

Kode Card ("the app") is a fully offline QR code and Wi-Fi card generator. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and what it does not. The short version: the app collects nothing, tracks nothing, and sends nothing anywhere.

2. NO DATA COLLECTION

The app does not collect, record or transmit any personal or non-personal data. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting services and no third-party trackers in the app.

3. NO PERMISSIONS

The app requests zero Android permissions. It cannot access your camera (it generates QR codes; it does not scan them), your contacts, your location, your microphone, your files or the internet.

4. INFORMATION YOU TYPE INTO THE APP

To make a QR code you may type information such as a Wi-Fi network name and password, a contact's name, phone number, email address, a website link or a message. This information is processed only on your device, at the moment you tap Generate, to draw the QR code image. It is never uploaded, sold, shared or sent to us or to anyone else.

5. HISTORY — STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The app keeps a History of the codes you generate so you can remake them later. History is stored in the app's private storage on your device only, and Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for the app, so History cannot leave your phone that way either. One important exception, by design: Wi-Fi passwords are NEVER written to History or to any storage. If you reopen a Wi-Fi entry from History, you will be asked to type the password again.

6. IMAGES YOU SAVE OR SHARE

When you tap "Save QR PNG" or "Save Card", the image is written to your device's Pictures/KodeCard folder using Android's media storage, which the app can do without any permission. When you tap "Share", Android's standard share sheet sends the image only to the app or person you choose. What an exported image contains is visible in the image itself: for example, if you choose to print the password on a Wi-Fi card, the password appears on that card image. Treat such images like the password itself.

7. NO INTERNET USE

The app contains no networking code and cannot connect to the internet. Features work fully offline, including in airplane mode.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. The app is a general-purpose tool suitable for all ages.

9. DATA SECURITY

Data that is never collected, transmitted or stored on a server cannot be leaked from a server. The information kept on your device (History and settings) is protected by Android's app sandbox: other apps cannot read it unless your device itself is compromised.

10. DELETING YOUR INFORMATION

You can delete individual History entries inside the app, clear all History from the Guide tab, or erase everything by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. Images you exported to your gallery are yours to delete like any other photo.

11. THIRD-PARTY LIBRARIES

The app includes the ZXing core library (Apache License 2.0) solely to perform QR code mathematics on your device. It performs no network communication and collects no data.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If a future version changes any of the above, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date changed before that version is released.
